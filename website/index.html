Set up your studio.

This preview is not Apple-notarized yet, so macOS needs one extra approval on first launch.

  1. 01

    Download the DMG

    Use the official download on this page, then open the file.

  2. 02

    Move it to Applications

    Drag Whisper Studio into the Applications folder.

  3. 03

    Try to open it once

    Open Whisper Studio from Applications. If macOS blocks it, close the warning.

  4. 04

    Choose Open Anyway

    Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, then choose Open Anyway and confirm.

Allow about 1 GB for the app and its first local model. Meeting recording also asks for Microphone permission when you use it.

A few things
worth knowing.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Download a transcription model during setup, then transcribe, edit, and export without an internet connection.

Which Macs are supported?

Version 0.5.21 requires macOS 15 or later and an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer).

What can I transcribe?

Audio and video files, including MP3, M4A, WAV, MP4, and voice notes. Language can be detected automatically.

Where do my files go?

Recordings and transcripts are saved locally on your Mac. You can manage them in your library and export documents, subtitles, or a transcript-and-media bundle. Connected cloud services are optional.

How do I install it?

Open the DMG and drag Whisper Studio into Applications. If macOS blocks the first launch, follow the beta installation guide above.
